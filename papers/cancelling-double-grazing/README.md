# Cyclicity Six and Saturation Geometry at Cancelling Double Grazing

[Paper](paper.pdf) · [Author source](source/) · [Mathematical supplement](supplement/paper.pdf) · [Supplement source](supplement/source/)

Public edition, 2026-10-27. Haibo Lu.

The main paper studies sharp cyclicity six, saturation scales, quartic
geometry and realization at cancelling double grazing. The supplement studies
secondary saturation and cusp geometry. Both retain the author’s AI disclosure.

See source/README.md and supplement/source/README.md for builds and finite
checks; repro/symbolic/README.md and repro/interval/README.md describe the
selected computational materials and their limits. Interval certificates were
not rerun during this edition preparation. No proof-assistant verification is claimed.
